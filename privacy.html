Privacy policy

Jarvis is a private assistant used only by its owner.

  • It asks for one Google permission: to create and manage calendars it creates itself. It cannot read or change any other calendar or event.
  • It only adds and removes reminder events in its own "Jarvis" calendar.
  • No Google data is sold, shared with anyone, or sent anywhere else. The sign-in token stays on the owner's own computer.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
